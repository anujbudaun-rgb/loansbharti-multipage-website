NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Understand the basics before sharing an enquiry.

Does loan consolidation guarantee a lower EMI?

No. The EMI depends on the approved amount, interest rate, tenure and fees. A lower EMI can also mean paying more interest over a longer period.

Will I definitely get an offer?

No. All offers are subject to lender policies, eligibility checks, verification and approval.

Will checking options affect my credit score?

It depends on the checks performed. Ask whether a lender or intermediary will make a soft enquiry or a hard credit enquiry before you consent.

What should I compare before accepting?

Compare APR/interest, processing fees, tenure, total repayment, insurance add-ons if any, foreclosure terms and the lender's official documents.
